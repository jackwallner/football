Scout the NFL like a pro.
Advanced percentile rankings and Next Gen-style metrics for every qualified player.

Gridiron StatScout brings NFL percentile rankings to your iPhone. See where players rank — from EPA per play and CPOE to YAC over expected and target share — with fast visual red-blue metric bars.

VISUAL PERCENTILES
Every metric displays as a horizontal percentile bar with a colored marker. The color shifts continuously from blue (0th percentile) through gray (50th) to red (100th), so you can spot elite performers instantly.

COMPREHENSIVE PLAYER PROFILES
Tap any player to see their full profile. View percentile rankings across Passing, Rushing, Receiving, and Defense. Switch between advanced metrics and standard season stats, and track recent form game by game.

NIGHTLY UPDATES
Our data feed refreshes every night, pulling the latest percentile rankings and standard stats for qualified players. What you see is always current through the previous week's games.

LEADERBOARDS & FILTERS
Browse the full league leaderboard filtered by Passing, Rushing, Receiving, or Defense. Sort and search across every active player. Tap any row to dive into their full profile.

GRIDIRON PRO

• Player Comparisons — compare any two players side by side
