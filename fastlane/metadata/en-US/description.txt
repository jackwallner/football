Pro Football: EPA StatScout is an NFL advanced stats app for iPhone. Rank players by EPA per play, CPOE and more, read a full percentile profile in one tap, and see win probability and EPA for every game. Current-season leaderboards, team pages and Following are free, with no account and no ads.

PLAYER PROFILES
• Every metric is a percentile bar ranked against players in the same season and season type
• Metrics are grouped into Passing, Rushing, Receiving and Defense
• Switch between advanced metrics and standard season stats
• Share a player's percentile card as an image
• Contract Value card for quarterbacks, running backs, receivers and tight ends, comparing production with pay

METRICS
• Passing: EPA per play, CPOE, interception rate, sack rate, time to throw, aggressiveness, intended air yards
• Rushing: EPA per rush, rushing yards over expected, explosive run rate, fumble rate
• Receiving: EPA per target, WOPR, target share, RACR, separation, yards after catch over expected
• Defense: pressures, hurries, QB knockdowns, completion percentage, yards and passer rating allowed, missed tackle rate (advanced defense covers 2018 onward)
• Familiar totals too: passing, rushing and receiving yards, touchdowns, tackles, sacks and interceptions

LEADERBOARDS AND FOLLOWING
• Rank players by advanced or standard stats for the regular season or playoffs
• Filter by QB, RB, WR, TE or Defense and by AFC or NFC, and search by name
• Follow your team and favorite players in one place

GAMES
• Each week's slate with final scores and projected margins
• Advanced box score for each finished game: win probability by play, team efficiency, plays that swung it, game leaders, team stats and the full box score
• Stats are checked for updates while the app is open, and the Stats, Teams and Trends screens show which week the numbers run through

TEAMS
• A page for each of the 32 clubs with advanced and standard team stats and a roster you can rank by any metric
• Division standings and StatScout Power Ratings

FREE AND STATSCOUT+
Free: current-season leaderboards, player profiles, Following, Games and box scores, team pages, standings and Power Ratings, and the top Contract Value row at each position.
StatScout+ adds:
• Trends: the league ranked by who is heating up and cooling off
• Recent Form: the last 3, 5 or 8 games for any player, club or leaderboard
• Compare: any two players or teams side by side, or one player year over year
• Best & Worst: the league leader and trailer on every metric
• Player efficiency in each game: EPA, success rate, CPOE and depth of target for every passer, rusher and receiver
• The full Contract Value boards
• Every season back to 2000, plus career percentiles since 2000
Choose Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime. Subscriptions include a free trial for eligible new subscribers, and the app shows its length before you start. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads and no cross-app tracking. The app only contacts the servers that supply NFL stats and handle purchases.
