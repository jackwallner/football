StatScout is a read-only NFL statistics viewer with no user accounts or sign-in.

Core flow:
1. Launch app - new users see onboarding; returning users land on the leaderboard
2. Scroll the leaderboard of qualified players with percentile rankings, filtered by QB, RB, WR, TE, DEF
3. Tap a player for a profile with visual percentile bars, standard stats, and year-over-year compare
4. Trends ranks the league by who is heating up or cooling off
5. Teams covers all 32 clubs, advanced and standard
6. Compare puts any two players side by side

StatScout+ features (IAP via RevenueCat):
- Trends, recent form, player comparisons, team scouting, seasons back to 2000, All-Time career percentiles
- Test with a Sandbox account via RevenueCat

Data: public nflverse / nflreadpy NFL statistics, refreshed nightly. Not affiliated with the NFL, any NFL team, or the NFLPA. No league, team, or player imagery is used anywhere in the app or the screenshots.

Known: no accounts, no social features, no live scores, no play-by-play, no projections or betting content.
