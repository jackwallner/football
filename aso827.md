## US keyword architecture

### Current field

```text
gen,epa,cpoe,yac,statistics,compare,player,passing,rushing,receiving,defense,qb,fantasy,teams,trends
```

The current field is exactly 100 bytes. Its structure is generally strong, but `fantasy` creates an expensive intent mismatch. A fantasy-football searcher expects projections, roster advice, start/sit decisions, waiver recommendations, drafts, or DFS. Football Next supplies descriptive NFL statistics and comparisons, not those decisions.

### Recommended field

```text
gen,epa,cpoe,yac,statistics,compare,player,passing,rushing,receiving,defense,qb,team,trends,scouting
```

This field is exactly 100 ASCII and UTF-8 bytes. It does not repeat `football`, `next`, `StatScout`, `advanced`, `NFL`, `stats`, or `analytics` from the title and subtitle.

The change is deliberately narrow:

- Remove `fantasy`.
- Change `teams` to `team` without losing useful combinations.
- Add `scouting` to describe the implemented player and team research job.

## Wrong-intent and authority walls

Do not target:

- `fantasy football`, `dfs`, `start sit`, `waiver wire`, draft, projection, or lineup terms
- scores, live, news, schedule, standings, highlights, play-by-play, or streaming
- odds, picks, props, gambling, betting, sportsbook, or prediction
- `NFL app`, official, authorized, endorsed, team app, or league app
- generic `sports analytics`, `football app`, `pro football`, or `american football` in US fields
- `PFF` or other competitor brands
- player-photo, team-logo, shield, or league-mark claims not cleared for use

The app may be useful to fantasy players, but utility is not the same as a fantasy product. Metadata should describe what users can do, not every downstream decision they might inform.

## Positioning and claims

Recommended position:

> Advanced NFL player and team statistics, shown as rankings, percentiles, trends, and comparisons

Safe claims:

- Rank qualified players by advanced or standard metrics
- Read player percentiles across passing, rushing, receiving, and defense
- Compare two players or one player's seasons
- Review recent form over the literal implemented window
- See team advanced and standard stats
- Explore historical seasons back to 2000 and all-time career percentiles where the data and qualification rules support them
- Receive nightly data refreshes only if the backend and cache freshness guarantee remain true

Do not claim:

- Live, real-time, in-game, or play-by-play data
- Predictions, projected performance, fantasy recommendations, betting value, or guaranteed insight
- Official NFL, NFLPA, club, player, or data-provider affiliation
- Every player when qualification filters exclude players
- `right now` when the capture shows a completed historical season
- A game-count recent window if the backend actually returns league weeks
- Complete or error-free data when coverage and refresh can vary

Keep the independent, non-affiliation disclaimer in description, terms, support, review notes, and any marketing page that uses NFL, team, or player identifiers.
