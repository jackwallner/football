# Football icon — percentile-slider family

Football sibling of the baseball StatScout icon: horizontal percentile tracks, each
filled to a different value, with the ball riding the leading edge of the fill like a
slider thumb. Generated by `scripts/generate_football_icon.py` (4x supersampled, LANCZOS
down to 1024, no alpha).

## What changed from the baseball original

- **Ball.** Circle becomes a prolate spheroid built as a true vesica (two circular arcs),
  so the tips are mathematically sharp instead of hand-cut. Aspect is 1.66:1, matching a
  real ball's 11" x 6.7" rather than the leaf-like 1.9:1 a naive ellipse gives. Tilted 24°
  so it echoes the ascending read of the stack. Flat `leather` with one very subtle
  vertical shade, computed per ball so every ball on the canvas is the same value (a
  canvas-wide gradient made the lower balls visibly darker than the upper ones).
- **Laces.** Cream spine plus four cross-stitches, all one stroke weight (~8.5px @1024,
  well clear of the 12px floor after the ball is scaled). Short and evenly spaced so they
  don't fuse into a white blob at 120px.
- **Halo.** The baseball's navy ring becomes a background-coloured knockout that traces
  the football outline. Keeps the ball reading as a thumb *on* the track rather than a
  sticker over it. I dropped the bar-coloured outer hairline the baseball icon has: on a
  pointed shape it read as a stray outline, especially against gold.
- **Colour.** The baseball's Savant red/blue becomes this app's own ramp, straight from
  `SavantDesign.swift`: `performanceHigh` → `turf` → `gold` → `performanceLow`. Same
  "these are percentiles" logic, none of the MLB colour language.
- **Pills kept.** `ICON_BRIEF.md` argues for squared 2px caps to match `radiusCard: 4`.
  Full pills are deliberate here, because the pill *is* the recognisable form of the
  sibling icon and the thing that reads as a slider. Flagging the deviation.

## Concepts

- **`concept_a_dark`** — four bars, green → turf → gold → rust on `midnight`. The direct
  translation of the baseball icon.
- **`concept_b_two_hue`** — four bars, two greens over two rusts, structurally the closest
  mirror of the baseball's red/red/blue/blue. **Weakest of the four:** the rust fills sit
  right on top of `leather`, so bars 3 and 4 and their footballs muddy together. Included
  for completeness.
- **`concept_c_cream`** — same ramp on `canvas`. Matches the app's actual light almanac UI
  and stands out against a dark-icon-heavy Home Screen. The cream halo does read slightly
  sticker-ish.
- **`concept_d_three_bar`** — three chunky bars, green → gold → rust. Fewer, bigger
  elements.

## Recommendation

**`concept_d_three_bar`.** The proof sheets decide it: at 60px and 40px the four-bar
versions collapse into stripes and the footballs become indistinct dots, while the
three-bar version still shows three separate tracks with recognisable balls on them. It
also lands the brief's "max three hues plus cream" exactly, and green → gold → rust is a
single legible heat ramp instead of four steps where two are nearly the same green.

`concept_a_dark` is the pick if matching the baseball icon bar-for-bar matters more than
small-size legibility.
