- Largest text: 3-letter team codes on the Stats board wrap ("SE/A") in the 44pt team column; header labels RANK and TEAM truncate; the onboarding pages need a scroll to reach the bullets. Readable and buyable, not pretty.
- September gap: a free user's first open in September now decodes the 34k-row history bundle to show last season (Pro users already do this). The season that just ended comes from the device's live cache or a bundle refreshed before the rollover (see `.claude/rules/backend-pipeline.md`); if neither has it, the board opens on an empty last season. Worth one check on 1 to 10 September 2027.
- B11: the reload after reconnect waits for the next foreground check or 2-minute tick, up to 5 minutes after the status throttle; a real airplane-mode toggle has not been run.
- B10 relies on RevenueCat delivering the lapsed CustomerInfo to the delegate in a long-lived process.
- B6: "Purchases restored." is drawn in the same turf colour as error text in that card.
