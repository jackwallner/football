mediumconfirmedstate
Impact

B11

After an offline launch, a returning user's empty Stats board never reloads on its own, even once the phone is back online

lowplausiblestate
Impact

A returning fan who opens the app once without signal sees an empty Stats tab for the rest of the session unless they tap Try Again. A fresh install does not have this problem because it has no saved revision. The automatic recovery the app advertises (the 2 minute status loop) does not apply here.

How it happens
  1. Use the app online once so a data revision is shown and saved (the shown revision is kept in UserDefaults).
  2. Get the saved player snapshot removed while the app is closed: iOS clears the Caches folder under storage pressure, or the app is updated from 1.2.1 or older, whose unmarked snapshot is deleted on first launch.
  3. Open the app with no connection (airplane mode). The live load fails and the Stats tab shows 'Connect to load 2026'.
  4. Turn the connection back on and leave the app open, or background and reopen it.
  5. The board stays empty. The 2 minute timer and the foreground check ask the status endpoint, which answers 'ready, same revision as the saved one', so the result is 'unchanged' and only the schedule reloads. Only the Try Again button or pull to refresh recovers.
