## More info

How it's built.

### The Pitch

Box scores tell you who got the volume. They don't tell you who was efficient, who was unlucky, or who is playing better than their yardage suggests.

StatScout puts the advanced numbers (EPA per play, CPOE, YAC over expected, rushing and receiving efficiency, defensive impact) next to the familiar totals, and renders every one as a percentile you can read at a glance. Sort the league and tap a name to see where a player stands.

### StatScout+

Leaderboards, filters, and full percentile profiles are free. StatScout+ is available monthly, yearly, or as a one-time lifetime purchase, with a 7-day free trial on subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

**Trends.** The league ranked by who is heating up and cooling off week to week.

**Recent form.** Last 3, 5, or 8 games applied to any player, club, or leaderboard.

**Comparisons.** Two players side by side, or one player's seasons against each other.

**Team scouting and history.** All 32 clubs, every season back to 2000, and all-time career percentiles.

### What Mattered

**Percentiles over raw numbers.** "91st percentile" is easier to read than "0.14 EPA per play", so the color bar is the main interface.

**One tap to the profile.** Any row in any list opens the full player page.

**How stats are published.** Each new source update is computed, checked, and published in one step, then served precomputed, so the app opens fast and works the same on stadium wifi.

**History in the bundle.** Older seasons ship inside the app, which keeps year-over-year comparisons instant and offline-friendly.

## Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the app.

## Is there an NFL stats app for iPhone with advanced stats?

Yes. Pro Football: EPA StatScout ranks every NFL player on advanced percentiles such as EPA per play, CPOE and YAC over expected, next to the familiar box-score totals.

## What are NFL percentile rankings?

They rank each player against the rest of the league on a 0 to 100 scale for every metric. StatScout shows each one as a color bar, from rust at the 0th percentile through sand at the 50th to turf green at the 100th.

## What is EPA in football?

EPA (expected points added) measures how much a play changes a team's expected points on the drive. StatScout shows EPA per play as a percentile so you can compare players quickly.

## How often are the NFL stats updated?

During the season, the app checks for newly published stats throughout the day and updates as soon as they arrive. Every screen shows which week and games the numbers include.

## Is Pro Football: EPA StatScout free?

Yes, leaderboards, filters, full percentile profiles and Following are free. The optional StatScout+ upgrade adds trends, recent form, player comparisons, team scouting and history back to 2000, and prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

## Where does the data come from?

The app uses nflverse weekly stats and Next Gen Stats. It is not affiliated with the NFL, any team or the NFLPA.
