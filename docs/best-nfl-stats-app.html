Best NFL stats app for iPhone (October 2026)

By Jack Wallner. Last updated October 19, 2026.

The best NFL stats app depends on what you mean by stats. Best for advanced percentiles such as EPA per play and CPOE with no account: Pro Football: EPA StatScout. Best for live scores, play-by-play and alerts: Superfan Sports. Best for the official league experience and video: the NFL app. Best for fantasy rankings and prop picks: PFF. Best for answering a one-off stats question: StatMuse.

How we compared. I read each app's App Store listing and pulled its rating, update date and supported devices from Apple's public lookup on October 12, 2026 (US storefront). I opened HOF on a Mac to see what a new user gets before signing in. The other apps are described from their listings, so I only state what a listing says. Jack Wallner makes Pro Football: EPA StatScout, so read its entries with that in mind.

Pro Football: EPA StatScout

StatScout ranks NFL players on advanced percentiles in Passing, Rushing, Receiving and Defense, with a red-to-green style bar on every metric, EPA per play, CPOE and YAC over expected among them. Leaderboards, full profiles, search and a Following list are free with no account and no ads, and it checks for new stats through the day in season. The trade-offs are real: it has no live scores, play-by-play, video, news, odds or fantasy tools, it is iPhone and iPad only, and trends, recent-form windows, comparisons, team scouting and seasons back to 2000 sit in the paid StatScout+ tier. It is new, with no ratings yet.

Superfan Sports: NFL Football

Superfan is the best pick here for following games as they happen. Its listing covers live scores, drive-by-drive play-by-play, instant alerts, standings, injuries, odds, win probabilities and box scores, with player stats, game logs and league leaders for passing, rushing, receiving, defense and kicking. Its stats are the traditional kind plus game analysis, and the listing does not mention EPA or CPOE. It has a large rating count and a high rating.

NFL

The official app is a hub for scores, highlights, news and the NFL Channel, with live games and replays in the NFL+ subscription. Its listing says NFL+ Premium includes NFL Pro, with over 95 player and team performance stats, and that this is available on desktop and mobile web. So the deepest league analytics are a subscription product that the listing places on the web rather than in the app. Choose it for the league's own video and coverage.

PFF: Betting & Fantasy Tools

PFF is built around fantasy and betting decisions: weekly rankings, a start and sit optimizer, a player prop tool built on PFF grades, a mock draft simulator and prospect scouting. Pick it if you play fantasy or study the draft. It is a decision tool more than a percentile browser, and much of it needs the PFF+ subscription.

StatMuse

StatMuse answers sports questions typed in plain English across the NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, college football, golf and European football. It is the fastest route to a single number, such as one player's total in a season. It is a search box, not a leaderboard or percentile browser, and its rating is the lowest in this list.

HOF: Sports Stats & Data

HOF is aimed at prop and parlay research, with hit-rate leaderboards, matchup grades and odds across 15 leagues. NFL is one of those leagues. It asked me to sign in through a web login before showing any content, so check that you are comfortable with that first.

What other NFL stats apps will you see?

One more app shows up when you search for an NFL stats app. I did not open it; the note below comes from its App Store listing as of October 13, 2026.

theScore: Sports News & Scores

theScore is a general sports app rather than an NFL stats tool. Its listing covers real-time scores and alerts, news, video, box scores with team and player stats, standings and league leaders across the NFL, college football and dozens of other leagues, plus a personalized favorites feed and fan chat. It suits fans who follow several sports and want scores and headlines in one place. The listing does not mention advanced metrics such as EPA or CPOE. It has a 4.82 rating from 857,439 ratings and was last updated October 10, 2026.

Which should you pick?

For advanced stats like EPA per play with no account, pick StatScout. For live scores and alerts, pick Superfan Sports.

  • You want to see who is elite in EPA per play, CPOE or YAC over expected, without an account: StatScout.
  • You want live scores, play-by-play and alerts: Superfan Sports.
  • You want video, highlights and the league's own app: NFL.
  • You play fantasy, bet props or study the draft: PFF.
  • You want one quick answer to a stats question: StatMuse.

Many fans use two: a scores app on Sundays and a stats app the rest of the week. New to the metrics? Start with what EPA per play means.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app for NFL advanced stats?

For advanced percentiles such as EPA per play, CPOE and YAC over expected on an iPhone with no account, Pro Football: EPA StatScout. The NFL's own NFL Pro stats are part of NFL+ Premium and, per the listing, are available on desktop and mobile web.

Which NFL stats app is best for live scores?

Superfan Sports: NFL Football lists live scores, play-by-play, alerts and box scores. The official NFL app also covers scores and adds video with an NFL+ subscription.

Is there an app for Pro Football Reference?

No. A search of the US App Store on October 12, 2026 found no app from Pro Football Reference itself. Sports Reference lists a Pro Football Draft Machine app, which is a draft tool.

Which app is best for fantasy football stats?

PFF lists weekly rankings, a start and sit optimizer and projections. StatScout is a stats browser and has no fantasy tools.

Do I need an account to use these apps?

StatScout needs no account. HOF asked me to sign in through a web login before showing content. For the others, the listing does not say.

What is the best app for football stats?

It depends on what you mean by stats. Pro Football: EPA StatScout ranks players on advanced percentiles such as EPA per play and CPOE with no account, Superfan Sports covers live scores, play-by-play and box scores, and the NFL app is the official home for video. PFF suits fantasy rankings and props, and StatMuse answers a one-off stats question.

What is the best site for NFL stats?

For a website, Pro Football Reference from Sports Reference is a free archive of current and historical NFL stats, with player, team and box score pages going back to 1920. The NFL's own NFL Pro, with over 95 player and team performance stats, is part of NFL+ Premium and runs on desktop and mobile web, per the NFL app's listing. On a phone, the apps above cover the same ground in a smaller window.
