Description of Service

StatScout provides visual percentile rankings and leaderboards for NFL players using publicly available play-by-play and Next Gen Stats data. The app is for informational and entertainment purposes only.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

StatScout offers the following optional Pro purchases, each of which unlocks the same Pro features (historical seasons, player comparisons, and year-over-year tracking):

  • Monthly subscription — auto-renewing, billed once per month.
  • Yearly subscription — auto-renewing, billed once per year, includes an introductory free trial for eligible new subscribers.
  • Lifetime — a single non-consumable purchase.

Pricing is displayed in the App Store and in the in-app paywall in your local currency before purchase.

Billing & Auto-Renewal

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and are charged to the payment method on your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.

Auto-renewing subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing period at the price disclosed at the time of purchase, unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.

You can manage your subscriptions and turn off auto-renewal at any time by going to your Apple ID account settings on your device (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions). Cancelling stops future renewals; access to Pro features continues until the end of the current paid period.

Free Trial

If you start the yearly subscription with a free trial, the trial automatically converts into a paid subscription at the trial's end unless you cancel before the trial ends. The free trial is available only to eligible new subscribers as determined by Apple. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
